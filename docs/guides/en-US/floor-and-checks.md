# Floor, checks and payments: quick guide

For servers, cashiers and managers. The **Tables** module must be turned on for your restaurant, and your
role decides which buttons you see.

> Dinequora **records** payments. It does not charge cards yet: run the card on your terminal, then record
> the payment here.

## 1. Set up the floor (managers)

1. Open **Tables** and pick your store in the store selector at the top.
2. **New section** (optional): Patio, Bar, Main room…
3. **New table**: a label (T1, Bar 3…) and the number of seats.

A table is **Available**, **Occupied**, **Reserved** or **Cleaning**. Change it from the table's menu.
A table with an open check can't be set back to Available by hand: close or move the check first.

## 2. Seat guests and open checks

1. On a free table: menu › **Seat guests…** › how many people › **Seat and open check**. You are recorded
   as the server.
2. **Separate checks at the same table:** inside the check, **New check** (optional name, like "Ana").
   Each check is paid on its own.
3. **Move the party** to another table: **Move to another table**. The old table goes to Cleaning.

## 3. Send rounds to the kitchen

1. Pick the check (tabs at the top), search the menu, tap items, choose options.
2. Optional note for the kitchen.
3. **Send to the kitchen**. The round appears on the KDS with the table and the check name, and the
   ingredients come out of stock **now**.

- **Void a round** (trash icon, managers): the kitchen ticket is cancelled and the ingredients go back to
  stock. Only while that check has no payment.
- **Move a round** to another check of the same table (arrows icon), while neither check has a payment.

## 4. Print the check

**Print check** prints an 80 mm pre-bill with items, subtotal, sales tax, total, any amount already paid,
and tip suggestions (15/18/20% on the subtotal).

## 5. Take payment

Under **Payment**:

- **How to charge:** the whole balance, **split by people** (equal shares; the first shares carry the odd
  cents) or **by items** (each item with its share of tax).
- **Method:** card, cash or digital wallet. For cash, type what the guest handed over to see the change.
- **Tip:** none, 15%, 18%, 20% or another amount. Tips are kept apart: they belong to the staff, they
  are not revenue.
- **Charge**. When the balance reaches zero the check closes; when the last check of the table closes,
  the table goes to Cleaning.

**Reverse a payment** recorded by mistake (managers with refund permission): the check goes back to its
previous balance. Give the money back outside the system. The reason is visible only to people who can
reverse payments.

## 6. History of a check

**History** shows everything that happened on the check, with times and names: opened, each round
sent, voided rounds, payments, reversals, moves, closed. Checks closed today are under **Today ›
Closed checks**, each with its history.

## 7. Close the day

The **Today** panel shows checks closed, what was received **by payment method** (cash, card,
digital) and the tips, plus the tips of each server. **Print day close** prints it on 80 mm paper to
close the drawer. Printed during the day, it says "Partial".
