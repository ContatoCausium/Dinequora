# Insights and purchasing: quick guide

For owners and managers. Every number comes from what was recorded in Dinequora (sales, recipes, stock,
purchase orders). Nothing is estimated: when data is missing, the screen says so.

## Insights

Pick the period (7, 30 or 90 days) and the store (or all stores).

- **Revenue, net result, food cost (COGS %), gross margin, low stock**, with the change against the
  previous period.
- **Operational diagnostics:** food cost against the 28–32% reference, low stock, missing recipes, and
  two alerts:
  - **Sales drop**: revenue down 15% or more against the previous period of the same length.
  - **Supplier price rise**: an item's unit price in the latest purchase order is 10% or more above the
    order before it. It shows the item, supplier, old and new price.
- **Quick answers:** tap a suggestion or type a question. They work without AI and show the evidence and
  the method of each number. "What do I need to buy this week?" and "Which dishes should I promote?" use
  the two tools below.

## Menu engineering (Insights)

Each dish sold in the period, classified by how much it sells (its share of units) and how much it leaves
per unit (average price sold minus the current recipe cost):

| Class     | Sells  | Margin | What to do                                     |
| --------- | ------ | ------ | ---------------------------------------------- |
| Star      | a lot  | high   | keep quality and its place on the menu         |
| Plowhorse | a lot  | low    | review portion, recipe cost or price           |
| Puzzle    | little | high   | feature it, suggest it at the table, rename it |
| Dog       | little | low    | rework it or take it off the menu              |

A dish without a recipe cost is listed apart and not classified: add its recipe under **Menu and
recipes**. At least two costed dishes with sales are needed to classify.

## Purchase forecast (Inventory › Purchase orders)

What to buy to cover the next **7 or 14 days**, from what sales actually used in the last 30 days:

- **Use / day** = what sales took from stock in 30 days ÷ 30 (adjustments and losses don't count);
- **Lasts** = on hand ÷ use per day;
- **Buy** = use per day × days + minimum − on hand, rounded up.

The items that run out first are at the top, with their supplier. Items at or below their minimum with
no sales use are listed apart, without a quantity: check them by hand.

## The AI assistant (when your restaurant turns it on)

Ask in English or Portuguese ("why did my food cost go up this week?"). It reads only what you can see,
answers from your recorded numbers (sales, best sellers, menu engineering, purchase forecast, low stock)
and never changes anything. It may make mistakes: check important numbers in Finance and Reports.
